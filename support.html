常见问题

提醒没有按时出现

请前往 iOS“设置”，确认已允许 BeeDay 发送通知，并检查专注模式、通知摘要及静音设置。修改提醒时间后，请重新保存纪念日。

如何添加桌面或锁屏小组件

长按主屏幕或锁屏，进入编辑模式后添加 BeeDay 小组件。小组件可用样式会因设备和 iOS 版本而异。

如何恢复购买

打开 BeeDay，前往“我的”→“升级 Pro”，使用“恢复购买”。请确保设备登录的是购买时使用的同一 Apple 账号。

iCloud 同步异常

请确认设备已登录 iCloud、iCloud Drive 已开启，并保持网络连接。同步由 Apple 的 iCloud 服务提供。

隐私与数据

BeeDay 默认将纪念日数据保存在你的设备上；启用 iCloud 后，相关数据可能通过 Apple iCloud / CloudKit 在你的设备间同步。BeeDay 不要求创建独立账号。

Frequently Asked Questions

Reminders are not appearing

Open iOS Settings and make sure notifications are enabled for BeeDay. Also check Focus, Scheduled Summary, and silent delivery settings. Save the event again after changing its reminder time.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, enter edit mode, and add a BeeDay widget. Available widget styles depend on your device and iOS version.

How do I restore purchases?

Open BeeDay, go to Profile → Upgrade Pro, and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

iCloud is not syncing

Make sure you are signed in to iCloud, iCloud Drive is enabled, and the device has a network connection. Sync is provided through Apple iCloud services.

Privacy and Data

BeeDay stores anniversary data on your device by default. If iCloud is enabled, related data may sync across your devices through Apple iCloud / CloudKit. BeeDay does not require a separate account.
