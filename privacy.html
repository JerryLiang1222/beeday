Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026 · Effective date: June 30, 2026

English 简体中文

This Privacy Policy explains how BeeDay, also known as 小蜜日子, handles your information when you use the app.

1. Who We Are

BeeDay is an anniversary countdown and reminder app that helps you record important dates, set reminders, display widgets, and use system features such as iCloud, notifications, Face ID, and Touch ID when you choose to enable them.

2. Information We Process

To provide the app's core features, BeeDay may process the following information on your device or through system services you authorize:

  • Anniversary content, including titles, dates, categories, colors, pinned status, reminder settings, notes, and photos you choose to add.
  • Local preferences, including countdown display mode, appearance mode, widget display scope, and cached Pro status.
  • Notification permission status, used to determine whether anniversary reminders or test notifications can be delivered.
  • iCloud sync status. If you enable and sign in to iCloud, Apple may sync your anniversary data across your devices through iCloud / CloudKit.
  • Purchase status, used by Apple StoreKit to verify Pro access, restore purchases, and manage subscription status.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

BeeDay is designed to store your anniversary data locally on your device by default. If you enable iCloud, related data may be synced through Apple's iCloud / CloudKit services. BeeDay does not intentionally operate a separate third-party server to collect your anniversary content.

5. Third-Party Services

BeeDay may use Apple system services, including StoreKit, CloudKit, UserNotifications, WidgetKit, ActivityKit, and LocalAuthentication. These services are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policies.

6. Tracking, Advertising, and Data Sale

BeeDay does not use your anniversary content for third-party advertising, does not sell your personal information, and does not use your data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

7. Retention, Export, Import, and Deletion

You may export anniversary data for backup and import supported backup files inside the app. You may delete individual anniversaries in the app, or remove local app data by deleting the app from your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, deletion and recovery may also be affected by Apple's iCloud sync behavior.

BeeDay keeps your local anniversary data until you delete it, remove the app, or replace it through an import operation. If iCloud sync is enabled, data may remain in iCloud according to your Apple ID and iCloud settings until it is deleted through the app or managed through Apple services.

8. Your Choices and Revoking Consent

You can manage permissions such as Notifications, Photos, Face ID / Touch ID, and iCloud in iOS Settings. You can also turn off optional features such as App Lock, Live Activities, widget filters, or iCloud sync where supported by the app and iOS. To request help with data access or deletion questions, contact us through the in-app Contact Us entry.

9. How We Protect Your Information

BeeDay does not sell your anniversary content to third parties. Local data is protected by the iOS app sandbox. iCloud data is handled through Apple's account and cloud security systems. BeeDay does not receive or store your biometric information when Face ID or Touch ID is used.

10. Children's Privacy

BeeDay is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a minor, please use the app under the guidance of a parent or guardian.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features, legal requirements, or review requirements change. The latest update date will be shown on this page.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the in-app Contact Us entry or email us at jerryliang1222@gmail.com.

小蜜日子隐私政策

更新日期：2026年7月20日 · 生效日期：2026年6月30日

本隐私政策用于说明你在使用“小蜜日子（BeeDay）”时，本应用如何处理你的信息。

一、我们是谁

小蜜日子（英文名 BeeDay，以下简称“本应用”）是一款纪念日倒计时提醒工具，帮助你记录重要日期、设置提醒、展示小组件，并在你授权后使用 iCloud、通知、Face ID 等系统能力。

二、我们收集和使用的信息

为了提供核心功能，本应用可能会在你的设备或你授权的系统服务中处理以下信息：

  • 纪念日内容：包括标题、日期、分类、颜色、置顶状态、提醒设置、备注及你主动添加的照片。
  • 本地偏好：包括倒计时显示单位、外观模式、小组件展示范围、Pro 状态缓存等设置。
  • 通知权限状态：用于判断是否可以发送纪念日提醒或测试通知。
  • iCloud 同步状态：当你开启并登录 iCloud 后，系统可能会将你的纪念日数据通过 Apple iCloud 在你的设备间同步。
  • 购买状态：通过 Apple StoreKit 校验 Pro 权益、恢复购买和订阅状态。

三、信息的存储方式

本应用优先将你的纪念日数据存储在本机设备中。若你启用 iCloud，相关数据会通过 Apple 提供的 iCloud / CloudKit 能力进行同步。本应用不会主动搭建第三方服务器收集你的纪念日内容。

四、系统权限说明

五、第三方服务

本应用可能使用 Apple 提供的系统服务，包括 StoreKit、CloudKit、UserNotifications、WidgetKit、ActivityKit、LocalAuthentication 等。这些服务由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理相关数据。

六、追踪、广告与数据出售

本应用不会将你的纪念日内容用于第三方广告，不会出售你的个人信息，也不会使用你的数据在其他公司拥有的 App 或网站中追踪你。

七、数据留存、导出、导入与删除

你可以在应用内使用导出功能备份纪念日数据，也可以通过导入功能恢复数据。你可以在应用内删除单个纪念日，或通过系统删除 App 的方式清除本地数据。若使用 iCloud，同步数据的删除和恢复可能受 Apple iCloud 同步机制影响。

本应用会保留你的本地纪念日数据，直到你主动删除、卸载 App，或通过导入操作替换数据。如果你开启 iCloud，同步数据可能会根据你的 Apple ID 与 iCloud 设置保留在 iCloud 中，直到你在 App 中删除或通过 Apple 服务进行管理。

八、你的选择与撤回授权

你可以在 iOS 系统设置中管理通知、照片、Face ID / Touch ID、iCloud 等权限。你也可以在 App 或系统支持的范围内关闭应用锁、锁屏实时活动、小组件筛选、iCloud 同步等可选功能。如需协助处理数据访问或删除相关问题，可以通过应用内“联系我们”入口反馈。

九、我们如何保护你的信息

本应用不会将你的纪念日内容出售给第三方。对于本地数据，我们依赖 iOS 系统沙盒机制进行隔离；对于 iCloud 数据，我们依赖 Apple 的账号与云端安全机制；对于 Face ID / Touch ID 验证，本应用不会获取或存储你的生物识别信息。

十、儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童主动收集个人信息。如果你是未成年人，请在监护人指导下使用本应用。

十一、政策更新

我们可能会根据功能变化、法律法规或审核要求更新本政策。更新后会在本页面显示新的更新日期。

十二、联系我们

如果你对本隐私政策或数据处理方式有疑问，可以通过应用内“联系我们”入口反馈，或发送邮件至 jerryliang1222@gmail.com。
